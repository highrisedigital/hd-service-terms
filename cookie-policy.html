Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This Cookie Policy applies to websites operated by Highrise Digital Ltd, including websites operated under our RecPress trading name.

Highrise Digital Ltd is registered in England and Wales under company number 09989726. Our registered-office and other corporate details are available on our Company Information page.

In this policy, Highrise Digital Ltd is referred to as “Highrise Digital”, “we”, “us” or “our”.

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies. Not every technology described below is used on every website we operate.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies can be used to make a website function correctly, provide security, remember preferences or collect information about how a website is used.

Similar technologies can also store information on, or access information from, your device. References to cookies in this policy include these similar technologies where appropriate.

How do we use cookies?

We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural profiling or conventional website analytics.

The cookies used on our websites support secure payments and protect our forms against spam and automated abuse. They are activated only when you interact with the relevant parts of a website.

Website analytics

We use Fathom Analytics to understand how visitors use our websites, including which pages are visited and how visitors arrive at a site.

Fathom does not use cookies or track visitors across different websites. It provides us with aggregated website statistics without creating persistent profiles of individual visitors.

Fathom temporarily processes limited technical information, such as an IP address and browser user-agent, to produce privacy-focused statistics and protect its service.

Further information is available in Fathom’s data journey and Fathom’s privacy policy.

Stripe payments

Stripe is used on websites operated under our RecPress trading name. It is not used on our other websites unless this policy is updated to say otherwise.

We use Stripe to provide and secure our online product stores and payment facilities.

When you interact with a store or payment process on a RecPress website, Stripe may place cookies or use similar technologies to:

  • process payments;
  • prevent and detect fraud;
  • protect payment and customer information;
  • authenticate users; and
  • keep Stripe’s payment services functioning correctly.

Depending on the Stripe services and payment options used, these technologies may include:

  • __stripe_mid: helps Stripe assess the risk associated with a payment and prevent fraud;
  • __stripe_sid: helps Stripe assess the risk associated with a payment during a browsing session;
  • m: supports Stripe’s fraud-prevention and risk-assessment systems; and
  • other Stripe cookies: Stripe may set additional authentication or payment-functionality cookies depending on the payment method or features selected, such as Stripe Link.

Stripe controls the precise duration of its cookies, which may vary according to the Stripe feature being used. Current information about the types and duration of Stripe cookies is available in Stripe’s Cookie Policy.

Google reCAPTCHA

We use Google reCAPTCHA on forms across our websites to distinguish genuine submissions from spam, bots and other automated or abusive activity.

When reCAPTCHA is activated, Google may set the necessary _GRECAPTCHA cookie and process limited technical information required to assess whether a submission is likely to be legitimate.

Depending on the reCAPTCHA configuration, browser and any existing relationship you have with Google, other Google cookies may also be present.

These technologies are used for website security and the prevention of spam, fraud and abuse. We do not use them for advertising or website analytics.

Further information is available in Google’s reCAPTCHA documentation.

Essential cookies

We classify the Stripe and reCAPTCHA cookies described above as essential where they are used solely to support secure payments, authenticate users, prevent fraud or protect our forms and websites from spam and abuse.

We do not ask for consent before using cookies or similar technologies that are strictly necessary to provide a service you request or maintain the security of that service.

If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising or similar technologies in the future, we will update this policy and provide an appropriate consent mechanism where required.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies using your browser settings. Please be aware that blocking essential cookies may prevent payment facilities, forms or other protected parts of our websites from working correctly.

Instructions are normally available through your browser’s privacy or cookie settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our websites, service providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on our websites, with its revision date shown above.
